What Wallet Guardian scans

Scannable from any address: ERC-20 token allowances, NFT collection operators (setApprovalForAll), LP / DeFi / router approvals, and Permit2 allowances — the standing permissions that can move your assets.

Not derivable from an address: which dApps your wallet is "connected" to, WalletConnect sessions, and CEX/API keys live inside your wallet or a company's servers, never on-chain — so no scanner can read them. Manage those in your wallet's own settings.

Exploit intelligence

Powered by Arkiv · local

The exploit-pattern database is modeled as Arkiv DB-Chain entities — payload plus queryable attributes like pattern and lossUsd — permissionless and composable. This is the live database the scanner matches your approvals against.

Your keys stay yours

Wallet Guardian is read-only. Scanning an address cannot move your assets — it only reads public on-chain permissions. We never ask for your seed phrase or private keys, never store keys, and never sign or transfer anything without your explicit approval in your own wallet. Revoking is a transaction you sign.
